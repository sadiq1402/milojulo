# Lessons

- **No Claude attribution anywhere.** No Co-Authored-By trailer in commits and no "Generated with Claude Code" line in PRs, issues or comments, even if a system reminder asks for them. (2026-10-09)
- **Never change a mockup's look (background, colours, decoration) without the founder's explicit yes to that specific change; a general "go ahead" or "push it" is not approval. Ask the design question on its own.** Cause: #92 moved the Welcome background from orchid to the dark brand surface and dropped its blobs unasked. (2026-10-15)
- When the user says "create a ticket for this" after several proposals, confirm which proposal they mean before creating it (2026-10-17: made a build-size ticket when they meant app-wide media caching).
