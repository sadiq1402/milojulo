Questions

Good to know.

What is MiloJulo?

An app for meeting new people near you who want the same kind of connection you do: friends, activity partners, dates or professional contacts. The name comes from the Hindi milna-julna, to meet and mingle.

Is it a dating app?

It can be, but it doesn't have to be. Dating is one Mode out of six. Most people come for friends and activities.

Who can join?

Anyone 18 or older with an email address. Sign-up is open once the beta starts.

What does it do with my location?

It uses your phone's location to show people and activities near you. Others only ever see a rough distance band, never your exact spot.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything is free during the beta.

Is there an iPhone app?

Android comes first. iPhone follows. Join the waitlist and we'll tell you when it's ready.

How do I delete my account?

In the app under Settings, or on the web at milojulo.co.in/delete-account. Your photos, chats and profile are erased.
