Delete your account

You can delete your MiloJulo account and its data here, without the app. In the app it's under Me → Settings → Delete account.

What happens

  • At once: your profile disappears from Discover, Activities and Circles, and your location is erased.
  • Within about an hour: your profile, photos, prompts, chats, chat media and sign-in are deleted for good.
  • Kept for a while: anything that was reported to us (and a copy of reported photos or media) is kept for 90 days as evidence, then deleted. Records we must keep by law are kept only as long as the law requires.

This can't be undone. You can sign up again later with the same email, as a new account.

1. Your email

We'll send a 6-digit code to the email you use for MiloJulo.

2. The code

Enter the 6-digit code we sent you.

3. Why are you leaving?

Optional, but it helps us. We keep your answer without your name or email.

Your account is being deleted

Your data will be gone within about an hour, and the app will sign you out. Thank you for trying MiloJulo.

Trouble? Write to support@milojulo.co.in from the email on your account and we'll delete it for you.
