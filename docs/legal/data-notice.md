# MiloJulo Data Notice

Last updated 27 October 2026. Effective from the date you accept it in the app.

This is MiloJulo's privacy policy: what we collect, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it,
and how to get it corrected or deleted. We wrote it in plain language because a notice nobody reads
protects nobody.

MiloJulo ([Legal entity name], [Postal address]) decides how your data is used. In the words of
India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the Data Fiduciary and you are the Data
Principal.

## What we collect and why

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **Email address** | To send your sign-in code and to answer you. Never sold, never used for marketing. |
| **Date of birth** | To check you are 18 or over and to show your age. We keep the date, because ages change. |
| **Profile, seen by other Members:** first name, 2 to 4 photos, two prompt answers, Modes, interests, when you are usually free; if you add them, profession, industry and career stage; in the dating Modes, your gender | To show you to people who might want to meet you, and to explain why you match. |
| **Preferences, seen by nobody else:** genders, age range and distance for the dating Modes; how far Discover looks | To decide who you see and who sees you. |
| **Location** (see below) | To find people, Activities and Circles near you and show distances. |
| **What you do here:** connection requests and notes, chats including photos, video and voice notes, Activities you post or join, Circles you join, blocks, reports, Media Consent choices, whether you met someone | To run those features, keep people safe and learn whether MiloJulo helps people meet. |
| **Push token**, if you allow notifications | To tell your phone that something arrived. |
| **Records of the documents you accepted and when** | To show what you agreed to. |
| **Technical logs** kept by our providers (for example sign-in times and IP addresses) | Security and investigating misuse. |

We do **not** ask for your religion, caste, community, employer or education, and there is no
field to put them in. We do not show ads and we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

## Where you are

When you allow location, we take one point from your phone, **move it 200 to 500 metres in a fixed
random direction**, and store only that moved point. We keep no history: each new reading replaces
the last. No Member, and no part of the app, can read that point. It is used only inside the
database to work out distances, and what anyone sees is a rounded band, such as "under 2 km" or
"about 5 km".

If your location has not been updated in the last 30 days, or you do not allow it, you do not
appear in Discover and Discover shows you nobody; your chats keep working. The point is deleted
when you delete your account.

## Why we are allowed to

We process your data on the **consent** you give when you join, by accepting this notice, the Terms
and the Community Guidelines, for the purposes in the table above. Location is used only if you
allow it on your phone. Separately, we may keep or share data where the law requires it, for
example to answer a lawful request from an authority. If you are in the European Union or the UK,
our legal bases are your consent, the performance of our contract with you (running the service
you asked for), and our legitimate interest in keeping MiloJulo safe (moderation and report
evidence).

## Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw consent in Settings, as easily as you gave it, one document at a time. Withdrawing
hides your profile until you accept again, because we cannot run the service without it. Withdrawal
does not undo processing that already happened. To stop entirely, delete your account.

## Who else handles your data

These companies process data for us, only to run MiloJulo, under their own data protection terms:

| Who | What they do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, accounts and sign-in | Mumbai, India |
| Cloudflare (R2) | Stores photos and chat media in a private bucket; links expire within minutes | Cloudflare's global network |
| Resend | Sends your sign-in code email | United States |
| Google Firebase Cloud Messaging | Delivers push notifications | Google's global network |

Some of these providers process data outside India. Indian law allows this except to countries the
Government has restricted, and none of these are. If you live outside India, your data is stored in
India and handled as this notice says.

We may also disclose data when Indian law, a court or a lawful government request requires it, or
to protect someone from serious harm.

## How long we keep things

| Thing | How long |
|---|---|
| Your account, profile, photos, preferences, connections and messages | Until you delete your account |
| Chat media (photos, video, voice notes) | 90 days, then deleted automatically; the text of the chat stays |
| A file attached to a report | Copied aside as evidence and kept 90 days from the report, or longer while the report is still open |
| Reports and moderation records | Kept after an account is deleted, so a pattern of harm is not erased by deleting an account |
| Your location point | Replaced by each new reading; deleted with your account |
| Security logs | About one year |

## Deleting your account

Delete it in the app: Settings → Delete account. If you no longer have the app, write to
support@milojulo.co.in from the email address you sign in with and we will delete it for you.

You disappear from Discover, chats, Activities and Circles immediately. Within about an hour, a job
deletes your photos and chat media from storage and then the account itself; your profile,
prompts, interests, location, connections, messages, Join Requests, Circle memberships and feedback
go with it. What survives on purpose is listed above: report records and report evidence.

## Your rights

You can:

- **Get a summary of your data**: Settings → Request a copy of my data. We send it to your email.
- **Correct or update it**: most of it you can edit yourself in the app; for the rest, write to us.
- **Have it erased**: delete your account, or ask us.
- **Withdraw consent**: in Settings (above).
- **Nominate someone** to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act: write to us with their
  name and email.
- **Complain to us**: write to our Grievance Officer (below).
- **Complain to the Data Protection Board of India** if you are not satisfied with our answer.

If you are in the European Union or the UK you also have the rights to object, to restrict
processing and to data portability, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

We answer requests and grievances within 30 days, and never later than 90 days.

## Children

MiloJulo is for people 18 and over only. We do not knowingly process data about anyone younger. If
you believe a Member is under 18, report them in the app or tell us, and we will close the account.

## Security

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our providers. Access rules in the database
stop anyone reading data that is not theirs; exact location can be read by no one; photos and
media are only reachable through short-lived links issued after checking you are allowed to see
them; and every moderation action is logged. No system is perfectly secure.

If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India without
delay, and follow up with what happened, how far it reached and what we changed.

## Changes

When this notice changes in a way that affects you, we publish a new version, show it to you in the
app and ask you to accept it before you carry on. Smaller corrections are made on this page with a
new "Last updated" date.
