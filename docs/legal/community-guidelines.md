# MiloJulo Community Guidelines

Last updated 27 October 2026. Effective from the date you accept them in the app.

MiloJulo works when people feel safe enough to meet a stranger for coffee. These guidelines are
what we expect of everyone here, and what we act on when it goes wrong. They are part of the
[Terms of Use](./terms.md), and they apply on your profile, in requests and notes, in chat, in
Activities and in Circles.

## Be a real person

Use your own first name, your own photos and your real age. One account each. No impersonating
anyone, no photos of someone else, no accounts run for a business or a group.

## Be decent

- **No harassment.** Not after a declined request, not after a chat goes quiet, not anywhere.
  "No" is a complete answer and needs no explanation. No bullying, intimidation, or insulting
  anyone for their gender.
- **No threats or violence**, and nothing that encourages anyone to harm themselves. If you are
  struggling, the Safety Centre in the app lists where to get help.
- **No hate.** Nothing attacking people for their religion, caste, ethnicity, race, nationality,
  disability, gender or sexuality, and nothing that stirs up hatred or violence between groups.

## Sexual content

- Nudity and sexual images are **never allowed** in profiles, prompt answers, Activities or
  Circles.
- In chat they belong only in a **Casual** connection: one that exists only between two people
  who both chose Casual, and only once both of you have allowed media in that chat (Media
  Consent). Either of you can withdraw that at any time, and then such images and video stop
  showing.
- **Never** share an intimate image of anyone without their consent, including edited, morphed
  or AI-made ones. Never send sexual content to someone who has not asked for it.
- **Nothing involving minors**, in any form, ever. We remove it, ban the account and report it to
  the authorities.

## Be honest

No scams, no fake profiles, no investment or crypto pitches, no asking Members for money, no
selling or advertising, no recruiting into schemes, no gambling, no spam, no harmful links or
files. Do not post things you know are false to mislead people.

## Respect privacy and other people's rights

What someone tells you in a chat stays there. Do not share anyone's photos, messages, contact
details or where they live. Do not post anything you do not have the right to, such as someone
else's photos or copyrighted work.

On Android the app blocks screenshots and screen recording in chat. On iPhone it cannot, and we
would rather tell you that than let you believe otherwise. Assume anything you send can be kept.

## Stay within the law

Nothing illegal, nothing that threatens India's unity, integrity, defence, security or
sovereignty, its relations with other countries or public order, and nothing that incites an
offence or obstructs an investigation.

## Meeting in person

Meet somewhere public the first time. Tell a friend your plan. Arrange your own way there and
back. Leave the moment something feels off. If you are in danger, call the local emergency number
(112 in India) first, then report the person in the app.

## What we do about it

**Reporting.** Every profile, photo, message and Activity has a Report button. Choose a reason
(fake or spam, harassment, inappropriate content, underage, felt unsafe, other) and add details if
you like. A copy of what you reported is kept for the moderator. Reports are read by a person, and
we aim to act within 24 hours. We never tell someone who reported them.

**Blocking.** Blocking hides you and the other person from each other everywhere: Discover, chats,
Activities and Circles. It ends your connection with them. They are not told. You can unblock in
Settings → Blocked people.

**Automatic hiding.** A profile, photo or Activity reported by three different Members within
seven days is hidden until a moderator has looked at it. Messages are never hidden automatically; a
person decides.

**Word filter.** Some words are refused outright in names, prompt answers and Activities. In chat
the message is delivered and flagged to moderators.

**What a moderator can do.** Take content down, restore it if the report was wrong, or ban the
account. Every one of those actions is written to a log that cannot be edited afterwards. **A ban
is permanent**: we have no way to reverse one today.

**Legal orders.** We remove content when a court order or a lawful government request requires it.

## If you think we got it wrong

Write to support@milojulo.co.in, or to our Grievance Officer at grievance@milojulo.co.in (details
and timelines in the [Terms of Use](./terms.md), section 13). A person reads every message. We
cannot promise to reverse a ban, but we will look at what happened and answer you.
