# MiloJulo Community Guidelines

**Draft, version 1. Not yet reviewed by a lawyer.** Last updated 26 October 2026.

MiloJulo works when people feel safe enough to meet a stranger for coffee. These guidelines are
what we expect of everyone here, and what we act on when it goes wrong. They are part of the
[Terms of Use](./terms.md).

## Be a real person

Use your own name, your own photos, and your real age. No impersonating anyone, no photos of
someone else, no accounts run on behalf of a business.

## Be decent

- **No harassment.** Not after a declined request, not after a chat goes quiet, not anywhere.
  "No" is a complete answer and needs no explanation.
- **No sexual content where it isn't wanted.** Nudity and sexual images are not allowed in
  profiles or Activities at all. In chat, they belong only in a Casual connection, which exists
  only between two people who both chose Casual and who have both allowed media in that chat.
- **Nothing involving minors**, in any form, ever. We report child sexual abuse material to the
  authorities and ban the account.
- **No hate.** Nothing attacking people for their religion, caste, ethnicity, nationality,
  disability, gender or sexuality.
- **No threats or violence**, including towards yourself. If you are struggling, the Safety Centre
  in the app lists where to get help.

## Be honest

No scams, no investment or crypto pitches, no asking Members for money, no selling, no recruiting
into schemes, no links to anything you would not want a moderator to open.

## Respect privacy

What someone tells you in a chat stays there. Do not share anyone's photos, messages, contact
details or where they live. On Android the app stops screenshots in chat; on iPhone it cannot, and
we would rather tell you that than let you believe otherwise. Assume anything you send can be
kept.

## Meeting in person

Meet somewhere public the first time. Tell a friend your plan. Arrange your own way there and
back. Leave the moment something feels off — you never owe anyone an explanation.

## What we do about it

**Reporting.** Every profile, photo, message and Activity has a Report. Reports are read by a
person, and we aim to respond within 24 hours. We never tell someone who reported them.

**Blocking.** Blocking hides you and the other person from each other everywhere: Discover, chats,
the Activity board, Circles. They are not told. You can unblock from Settings → Blocked people.

**Automatic hiding.** A profile, photo or Activity reported by three different Members within
seven days is hidden until a moderator has looked at it. Messages are never hidden automatically;
a person decides.

**Word filter.** Some words are refused outright in names, prompt answers and Activity text. In
chat the message is delivered and flagged to moderators, because stopping a message mid-conversation
tends to hurt the person being harassed more than the person harassing.

**What a moderator can do.** Take content down, restore it if the report was wrong, or ban the
account. Every one of those actions is written to a log we cannot edit afterwards. A ban is
permanent: we have no way to reverse one today.

## If we get it wrong

Write to support@milojulo.in. A person reads it.
