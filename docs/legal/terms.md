## What MiloJulo is

MiloJulo helps you find people nearby whose intent matches yours — friends, activity partners,
people to date, people to work with — and nudges you towards meeting in real life. You tell us
what you are looking for (your Modes), we show you people within the distance you choose, and
either of you can ask to connect. A chat opens only when both of you agree.

MiloJulo is an introduction service. **We do not vet, interview or background-check anyone.**
Email verification means someone could read a code sent to that address; it says nothing about who
they are. Treat everyone you meet here as a stranger, because they are.

## Being 18 or over

MiloJulo is for adults. You must be 18 or older, and you tell us your date of birth when you join.
If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it.

The Casual Mode is explicitly about adult intent. It only appears between two people who have both
chosen it, and images and video in those chats need both people to allow them first. Everything in
the Community Guidelines about what may not be sent applies there too.

## Your account

- One account per person. Use your own name and your own photos — photos that show you.
- Keep your email address secure. Anyone who can read your email can sign in as you.
- You are responsible for what you post and send.

## What you may not do

The [Community Guidelines](./community-guidelines.md) are part of these terms and say this in
detail. In short: no harassment, no sexual content outside what Casual allows, no nudity involving
anyone who has not consented, no content involving minors, no hate speech, no threats, no scams,
no selling, no impersonation, no spam, no soliciting money, and nothing illegal.

Do not scrape MiloJulo, automate accounts, or try to get around blocks, bans or the rate limits.

## Meeting people

Meetings arranged through MiloJulo are between you and the other person. We are not there, we do
not supervise, and **we are not responsible for what happens.** Read the safety advice in the app
before a first meeting: meet somewhere public, tell a friend where you are going, arrange your own
way there and back, and leave if anything feels wrong. You never owe anyone an explanation.

person reviews every report.

**A ban is permanent.** We do not currently have a way to reverse one, so we do not promise an
appeal. If you think we have got it wrong, write to us at the address below and we will read it.
