# Overview

## The idea
MiloJulo helps people find and meet others who match their intent, interests, lifestyle and vibe: friends, a hangout, an activity partner, a community, a date, or professional contacts. People do not always want a date, and not always a lifelong friend. Sometimes they want someone with the same vibe for coffee, badminton or a trek.

Core positioning: one modern platform for every kind of intentional connection. Intent is the starting point, not dating.

Vision: the most trusted way to discover people, communities and real-world experiences that match your vibe. Move social discovery from endless scrolling toward meaningful real-world connection.

## Name and tagline
- Name: MiloJulo. Echoes Hindi *milna-julna* (to meet people, to mingle socially).
- Tagline: "Find your people."
- Other lines in the blueprint: "Same vibe. New people." / "Meet people. Make plans." / "More than dating."

## Brand promise (what users should feel)
- I can find people like me.
- I know what this person is looking for.
- I can meet people without feeling awkward.
- I have control over my privacy.
- This app helps me actually do something, not just chat.

## Brand personality
Modern, playful, inclusive, safe, social, non-judgmental, Indian but not traditional, premium but approachable. Energetic but not childish.
Avoid: hearts and cupid imagery, matrimony-style visuals, childish characters.

## Audience and place
Pune first, with open sign-up (decision 27). Young adults (18+) in the IT corridors and student areas: Hinjewadi, Wakad, Baner, Balewadi, Kharadi. Beta starts in two neighbourhood clusters, a few hundred approved users.

## Modes
Friends, Activities, Dating, Serious, Casual, Networking, plus Circles (small local communities). Discovery only shows people whose chosen modes overlap with yours. Casual carries an explicit adult-intent label and consent-aware messaging.

## North Star
Weekly meaningful connections: connections that both people mark as worth keeping after meeting.

## What V1 must prove
That people matched on intent and vibe will turn a match into a real meetup. The product is built around a plan to do something (the activity board), not around profiles and chat alone.

## Later (not V1)
Monetisation is deliberately postponed: premium features, ticketed events and venue partnerships are the likeliest paths, after retention is proven. AI features (embeddings, icebreakers, moderation) come after V1; matching is rule-based SQL in V1.
