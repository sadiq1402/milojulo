**MILOJULO**

**Find your people.**

Startup Concept & Business Blueprint
A modern social connection platform for friendship, hangouts, dating, activities and communities.

**Pune-first • India-ready • Community-led • Safety-first • AI-assisted**

**The idea in one sentence**
MiloJulo helps people find and meet others who match their intent, interests, lifestyle and vibe — whether they want friends, a date, a hangout, an activity partner, a community or professional connections.

# 1. Executive Summary

MiloJulo is a social discovery and connection platform built around a simple observation: people do not always want a date, and they do not always want to make a lifelong friend. Sometimes they simply want someone with the same vibe to go for coffee, watch a movie, play badminton, go to the gym, explore a new café, attend an event or build a genuine relationship.

Instead of making dating the default, MiloJulo makes the user's intention the starting point. Users choose what kind of connection they want, discover compatible people or activities, and decide how much of their identity they want to reveal.

**Core positioning**
One modern platform for every kind of intentional connection.

# 2. Vision

Build the most trusted platform for discovering people, communities and real-world experiences that match your vibe.

Long-term vision:

Make meeting new people in a new city as easy as ordering food or booking a ride.

Create a trusted social graph based on interests, intent, activities and experiences.

Move social discovery from endless scrolling toward meaningful real-world connection.

Become a local-to-global social infrastructure layer for cities.

# 3. Mission

Help people find their kind of people — safely, intentionally and without forcing every connection into a dating box.

# 4. The Problem

Moving to a new city makes it difficult to build a social circle after college.

Young professionals often have colleagues but lack friends outside work.

Dating apps dominate the online connection category even when the user only wants friendship or activities.

Existing social networks are good for following people but poor at helping strangers safely meet around a shared intention.

People often discover that they want different things only after starting a conversation.

Finding someone for a specific activity — gym, badminton, trek, movie, café, gaming or a weekend plan — is still fragmented.

Users need stronger privacy controls when meeting new people online.

# 5. The Opportunity

India has a large and increasingly mobile young-professional population. Cities such as Pune, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Hyderabad continually attract students and professionals who need new social circles. This creates an opportunity for a product positioned around connection rather than only dating.

| Existing category | What it does well | Gap MiloJulo targets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dating apps | Romantic discovery | Friendship, activities, communities and mixed intent |
| Social media | Content and following | Intentional real-world connection |
| Meetup/event platforms | Events | Person-to-person matching around intent |
| Messaging apps | Communication | Discovery and compatibility before messaging |
| Professional networks | Career identity | Human connection beyond work |

# 6. MiloJulo USP

**Primary USP**
MiloJulo is not a dating app with a friendship mode. It is a connection platform where the user chooses the type of connection first.

The product differentiates through six pillars:

| USP pillar | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Intent-first | Users explicitly select whether they want friends, dating, serious relationships, casual connections, activities, hangouts, networking or communities. |
| Vibe-first | Compatibility considers lifestyle, interests, personality, activities and social style — not only age and distance. |
| Activity-first | Users can connect around a real plan instead of only a profile: coffee, movie, gym, sport, trek, gaming, food, event, etc. |
| Progressive identity | Users control how much identity is revealed and can start from compatibility rather than a full personal profile. |
| Community-led | Local circles, events and recurring activities create network effects beyond one-to-one matching. |
| Safety-first | Verification, privacy controls, reporting, moderation and first-meet safety are built into the product. |

# 10. End-to-End User Journey

Download MiloJulo.

Complete 18+ age gate and phone/email verification.

Select city and approximate area.

Choose one or more connection intentions.

Select interests, activities and lifestyle preferences.

Create a profile with photos, prompts and optional AI assistance.

Set discovery, privacy and workplace visibility preferences.

Discover people, groups and activities.

See compatibility and “Why you match”.

Send a connection request, like or join an activity.

After mutual acceptance, chat opens.

Use optional AI icebreakers.

Move the connection into an activity/event/meetup.

After meeting, choose: keep connection, mute, block or disconnect.

# 12. Matching Engine

MiloJulo should not rely on one mysterious “compatibility percentage.” It should use multiple dimensions:

| Dimension | Example signals |
| --- | --- |
| Intent | Friendship vs dating vs activities |
| Lifestyle | Gym, travel, food, social habits |
| Interests | Movies, music, gaming, books |
| Activities | Badminton, trekking, café, gaming |
| Personality | Quiet/social, communication style |
| Location | Approximate practical distance |
| Availability | Tonight, weekend, weekdays |
| Relationship preferences | Long-term, exploring, casual |
| Professional | Industry, career stage, networking intent |

A match card should explain the recommendation in human language, for example: “You both want friendship, like badminton and cafés, work in tech, and are available on weekends.”

# 15. Safety, Trust & Privacy

18+ only.

Phone/email verification.

Optional selfie/liveness verification.

No exact live location on profiles.

Block, mute and report available everywhere.

Anti-bot, anti-spam and suspicious-behaviour detection.

Clear community rules and moderation.

Explicit intent labels for adult/casual connections.

Consent-aware messaging and connection controls.

Optional trusted-contact / first-meet safety workflow.

Do not reveal personal phone numbers automatically.

Privacy controls for workplace, community and profile visibility.

Safety should be a differentiator. A user should feel that MiloJulo is a place to meet new people without sacrificing control.

# 27. Validation Plan Before Heavy Development

Interview at least 30–50 people in the target age range.

Ask what they currently use to make friends or meet people.

Identify their top three desired connection types.

Test 3–5 brand/name directions.

Create a simple landing page and waitlist.

Run at least 3 small offline events.

Measure registrations, attendance, repeat attendance and referrals.

Build the MVP only after evidence that users will return for connections.

# 30. Founder Strategy

For a solo technical founder, the biggest mistake would be spending months building a full social network before proving demand. The first competitive advantage should come from community density and local execution.

Own one city before trying to own India.

Own one or two use cases before supporting every possible use case.

Build community and events alongside the app.

Talk to users every week.

Use the first 100–500 users as a product research group.

Treat trust and safety as core engineering work.

# 33. Final Startup Thesis

MiloJulo can become much bigger than a dating app if it successfully owns the problem of “I want to meet people like me.” The winning product is not another swipe interface. It is a trusted social discovery layer that understands intent, vibe, activities and context, then turns that understanding into real-world connections.

The initial strategy should therefore be narrow and disciplined: launch in Pune, build a dense community, start with friends + activities + hangouts, prove that users meet through the platform, then expand into dating, communities, premium features and additional cities.

**One-line startup thesis**
MiloJulo helps people find the right people for the right kind of connection at the right time.
